Predictive Digital Twin for SAG Mill Energy Efficiency in Copper Mining

A digital twin of a copper SAG mill: a Kalman filter fuses noisy ore-hardness sensors, gradient boosting predicts specific energy and throughput, and the energy bill is priced with real marginal costs from 95 monthly reports of Chile's energy commission. The plant is simulated, because no public SAG mill telemetry exists; the energy prices and the concentrator benchmark are real.

7.10 Real-data anchoring: energy price and concentrator consumption

The mill's energy is priced at the real marginal cost of the Crucero 220 kV bus, read from 95 monthly reports of the Comisión Nacional de Energía (2018-02 to 2026-08), including image-only reports read with OCR. Where a month is both published and implied by another report's percentage change, the two agree within 1.8% (98 pairs). Two reports that copied the previous month's figures were detected and discarded.
